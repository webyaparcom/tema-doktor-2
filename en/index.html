    Visit guide

    Before, during and after

    In an emergency call 112; the clinic is not a substitute for an emergency department.

    01Before the visit

    • event

      How is an appointment confirmed?

      Your chosen time is sent as a request; the front desk calls you to confirm.

    • badge

      What should I bring?

      Bring your ID, a list of the medicines you take and any previous test results.

    02During the visit

    • timer

      How long does a visit take?

      Slots are 20 minutes; an extra appointment is planned if needed.

    • family_restroom

      Can someone come with me?

      Yes, one companion may join you in the examination room.

    03After the visit

    • lab_research

      How do I get my test results?

      Results are reviewed with the doctor at the follow-up appointment.

    • emergency

      What if my symptoms get worse?

      In an emergency call 112; the clinic is not a substitute for an emergency department.
